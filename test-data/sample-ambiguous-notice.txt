FORMAL NOTICE: CODE COMPLIANCE DEFICIENCY & ADMINISTRATIVE ADVISORY

Dear Property Owner,

An official exterior property inspection conducted by this Department revealed non-compliance with Riverdale Municipal Code regarding unpermitted structural modifications and sidewalk maintenance adjoining the subject property.

REQUIRED TIMELINE:
Prompt compliance and response is required without delay. Corrective repairs must be commenced within a reasonable timeframe following formal receipt of this notice.

FINANCIAL ASSESSMENT & FEES:
Failure to initiate corrective action may result in administrative assessment fees. Additional supplementary penalties, inspector remediation charges, and municipal processing fees may apply in accordance with Municipal Enforcement Code Section 4B. The exact aggregate fee assessment will be determined following a secondary compliance inspection.

MANDATORY NEXT STEPS:
1. Contact the Department of Code Enforcement at (555) 019-2831 to schedule a preliminary compliance conference.
2. Submit a written remediation plan or permit application prior to initiating exterior reconstruction.
3. If you believe this notice was issued in error or wish to request an administrative variance, submit an appeal form in writing to the Municipal Board of Appeals.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining neighborhood safety standards.
